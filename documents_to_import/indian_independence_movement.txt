Indian Independence Movement

The Indian independence movement was the struggle to end British rule in India.

Key events
1. 1857: The Revolt of 1857 was the first large uprising against the British East India Company.
2. 1885: The Indian National Congress was founded.
3. 1920: Mahatma Gandhi launched the Non-Cooperation Movement.
4. 1930: The Salt March (Dandi March) began on 12 March as part of civil disobedience.
5. 1942: The Quit India Movement was launched with the call "Do or Die".
6. 1947: India became independent on 15 August 1947.

Important leaders
Mahatma Gandhi, Jawaharlal Nehru, Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel, Subhas Chandra Bose and Bhagat Singh.
