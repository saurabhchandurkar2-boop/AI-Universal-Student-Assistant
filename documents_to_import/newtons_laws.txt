Newton's Laws of Motion

Newton's laws of motion describe how forces change the motion of objects.

First Law (Law of Inertia)
An object stays at rest or moves in a straight line at constant speed unless an unbalanced force acts on it.

Second Law
The force on an object equals its mass times its acceleration.

The formula is:

F = m × a

Where:
F = force in newtons
m = mass in kilograms
a = acceleration in metres per second squared

For example, a 5 kg object accelerated at 2 m/s² needs a force of 10 N.

Third Law
For every action there is an equal and opposite reaction.
