Equations of Motion (Kinematics)

The equations of motion describe an object moving with constant acceleration.

Where:
u = initial velocity in metres per second
v = final velocity in metres per second
a = acceleration in metres per second squared
t = time in seconds
s = displacement in metres

First equation (final velocity)

v = u + a × t

Second equation (displacement)

s = u × t + ½ × a × t²

Third equation (velocity from displacement)

v² = u² + 2 × a × s

For example, a car starts from rest (u = 0) and accelerates at 2 m/s² for 5 seconds.

v = 0 + 2 × 5 = 10 m/s
s = 0 × 5 + ½ × 2 × 5² = 25 m

Therefore, the final velocity is 10 m/s and the displacement is 25 m.
