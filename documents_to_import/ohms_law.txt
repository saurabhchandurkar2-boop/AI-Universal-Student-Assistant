Ohm's Law

Ohm's law describes the relationship between voltage, current, and resistance in an electrical circuit.

The formula is:

V = I × R

Where:
V = voltage in volts
I = current in amperes
R = resistance in ohms

The current can be calculated using:

I = V / R

For example, if a circuit has a voltage of 10 V and a resistance of 5 ohms:

I = 10 / 5
I = 2 A

Therefore, the current is 2 amperes.
