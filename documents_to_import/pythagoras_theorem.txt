Pythagoras Theorem

In a right-angled triangle, the square of the hypotenuse equals the sum of the squares of the other two sides.

The formula is:

a² + b² = c²

Where:
a and b = the two shorter sides
c = the hypotenuse, the side opposite the right angle

For example, if a = 3 and b = 4:

c² = 3² + 4² = 9 + 16 = 25
c = 5

Therefore, the hypotenuse is 5 units.
